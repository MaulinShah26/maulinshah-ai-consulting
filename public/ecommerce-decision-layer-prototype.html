Why this is not another shopping chatbot

Conversation can be the input. It should not hide the decision.

A chat assistant can answer questions and recommend products. The decision layer makes the priorities, eliminations, trade-offs, evidence and uncertainty visible — and lets the shopper change them.

AI shopping assistant“What should I buy?”Conversation → answer → recommendation. Helpful, but much of the reasoning can remain inside the assistant.
Decision layer“What makes this right for me?”Context → decision model → ruled-out options → trade-off frontier → evidence → what changes the answer.
